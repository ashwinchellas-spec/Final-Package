These are academic model predictions, not measured engine tests.
Altitude_m is geometric; engine limits are checked in geopotential metres.
Mach and throttle are held fixed; true airspeed changes with ISA temperature.
Invalid points remain in CSV with Valid=0 and reasons; plots show gaps.
Compare jet thrust and TSFC within jet types, shaft power and BSFC within propeller types.
TSFC_kgNs and BSFC_kgkWh have different units and cannot be directly ranked.
Kaveri and optional piston records are estimates/assumptions, not verified variant data.
